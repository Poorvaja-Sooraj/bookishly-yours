# 1. Project Vision

## Project Name

Bookishly Yours

---

## Tagline

Your Personal Reading Memory Vault

---

## Vision Statement

Bookishly Yours is a personal reading memory vault designed for readers who want to preserve more than just the books they have read.

Most reading applications help users track pages, ratings, and reading goals. While these features are useful, they often fail to preserve the emotional journey that readers experience while reading a book.

Every book creates memories.

* A quote that made you stop reading for a moment.
* A character that felt real.
* A chapter that made you cry.
* A plot twist that left you speechless.
* A lesson that stayed with you.

Months later, those emotions begin to fade.

Bookishly Yours exists to preserve them.

It allows readers to collect everything that made a book meaningful—favorite quotes, highlighted pages, personal reflections, voice recordings, reviews, reading progress, and cherished moments—in one place.

Rather than asking,

> "How many books have you read?"

Bookishly Yours asks,

> "How did this book become part of your life?"

Our mission is simple:

> Help readers remember not only the stories they read, but also the person they were when they read them.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Readers often remember that they have read a book, but gradually forget the emotions, meaningful quotes, personal reflections, and experiences associated with it.

Existing reading applications mainly focus on statistics such as reading progress, ratings, reading goals, and recommendations. While these features are valuable, they rarely help readers preserve their personal connection with a book.

Readers often wish to revisit the emotions they experienced while reading, but memories fade over time, and scattered photos, notes, or screenshots stored across different apps make it difficult to relive those moments.

There is currently no simple and dedicated space where readers can preserve their complete reading journey in a meaningful and organized way.

---

# 3. Proposed Solution

Bookishly Yours provides readers with a dedicated personal space to preserve every meaningful aspect of their reading journey.

The platform combines reading progress tracking with personal memories such as:

* Favorite quotes
* Highlight page photos
* Personal reviews
* Private notes
* Voice recordings
* Reading statistics
* Reflections after finishing a book

Each completed book becomes its complete Reading Journey—a digital collection of memories that allows readers to revisit not only the story but also their own thoughts, emotions, and experiences whenever they choose.

---

# 5. Project Goals

The primary goals of Bookishly Yours are:

* Preserve readers' emotional experiences with every book.
* Organize all book-related memories in one place.
* Help readers revisit books months or years later.
* Encourage thoughtful and reflective reading.
* Create a personal digital reading journal.
* Provide an enjoyable and distraction-free reading companion.
* Build a beautiful and intuitive user experience.

---

# 6. Non-Goals (Version 1)

To keep the project focused and achievable within the planned timeline, the following features are intentionally excluded from Version 1:

* Public social feed
* Public reviews
* Friend system
* Book recommendations
* AI-powered chatbot
* Reading community
* Marketplace
* Audiobook player
* E-book reader
* Book purchasing

These features may be considered for future versions.

---

# 7. Success Criteria

Version 1 of Bookishly Yours will be considered successful if users can:

* Create an account.
* Build their personal library.
* Track reading progress.
* Add favorite quotes.
* Upload images from books.
* Record thoughts.
* Record voice notes.
* Write reviews.
* Save personal reflections.
* Rate books.
* Organize completed books.
* Revisit their Reading Journey at any time.

---

# 8. Core Philosophy

Every feature developed for Bookishly Yours must answer one question:

> "Does this help readers preserve or relive their reading experience?"

If the answer is Yes, the feature belongs in the product.

If the answer is No, it should be reconsidered or moved to a future release.

This philosophy will guide every design decision, feature request, and development milestone throughout the project.

---

# 9. Project Scope (Version 1)

Bookishly Yours Version 1 focuses on creating a private digital memory vault for readers.

The application will provide:

* User authentication
* Personal library management
* Reading progress tracking
* Quote management
* Photo uploads
* Thoughts and voice recordings
* Personal reviews
* Personal reflections
* Book ratings
* Reading statistics
* Reading Journey

The application will prioritize privacy, simplicity, emotional connection, and ease of use over social interaction or content discovery.
