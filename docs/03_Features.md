# 13. Core Feature

The defining feature of Bookishly Yours is the **Reading Journey**.

Unlike traditional reading applications that focus primarily on progress tracking and ratings, Bookishly Yours enables readers to preserve their emotional journey through thoughts, voice recordings, photos, quotes, reflections, and reading statistics in a single chronological experience.

The Reading Journey represents the identity of the application and differentiates it from conventional book tracking platforms.
